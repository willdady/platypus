# Boards

A **Board** is a Kanban board scoped to your Workspace: columns you arrange
left-to-right, and cards you drag between them. Boards are a shared working
surface — both you and your Agents can read and update the same board, which
makes them a natural place for an Agent to track tasks, triage items, or hand
work back to you.

## Create a board

<Steps>

### Open the board form

From your Workspace, go to the **Boards** section and click **Create board**.

### Name it and add labels

- **Name** — what to call the board (1–100 characters, unique within the
  Workspace).
- **Description** _(optional)_ — up to 500 characters.
- **Labels** _(optional)_ — add colour-coded labels (each has a name and a colour
  from a fixed palette) that you can later apply to cards. Use **Add label** for
  each, and the delete button to remove one.

Save to create the board. You can edit the name, description, and labels anytime
from the board's **Settings** (the gear icon).

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Deleting a label from a board also removes it from every card that was using
  it. The cards keep everything else — title, description, assignee — and there
  is no undo for the label itself.
</Callout>

## Work the board

The board view scrolls horizontally across its **columns**. From here you:

- **Add columns** with the dashed **Add column** button at the end of the row.
  Column names must be unique on the board.
  A column's three-dot menu holds exactly four items: **Move column left**,
  **Move column right**, **Edit** (which is where you rename it), and
  **Delete**. **Delete** refuses a column that still holds cards — move or
  delete them first.
- **Add cards** with **Add card** at the bottom of a column.
- **Drag** cards between and within columns, and reorder columns. On a phone
  you can't drag: use the card's **Column** field and the column menu's **Move
  column left** / **Move column right**.
- **Switch boards** from the name dropdown in the header.

Click a card to open its detail dialog, where you can edit:

- The title and description (the description supports Markdown).
- **Column**, **Labels**, and **Priority** (None, Low, Medium, High, Urgent).
- **Due Date** and a single **Assignee** — yourself or an Agent.
- **Comments** — a discussion on the card (also Markdown).

You can edit or delete only your own comments, unless you are an Org Admin.
That includes comments an Agent wrote: the **Delete** button is there, but the
request is refused.

Below the comments, **History** lists how the card got here — newest first. It
starts collapsed; click it to open. On a phone, **Details**, **Comments** and
**History** are separate tabs.

<Callout type="info">
  Cards record who created and last edited them — and that can be an **Agent**,
  not just a person. When an Agent works a board, its activity shows up in the
  card metadata and comments just like a teammate's.
</Callout>

## Agents and boards

Grant an Agent the **Kanban** tool set (in the [Agent form](/building-with-platypus/agents)'s
**Tools** card, under _Productivity_) and it can operate boards directly. The tool
set lets an Agent:

- **list boards** and **read a board's state** (columns and card summaries),
- **list Agents** it can assign cards to,
- **read, create, and update cards** — title, description, labels, priority, due
  date, and assignee,
- **move cards** between and within columns, **copy** and **delete** cards, and
  **bulk-edit** up to 30 cards at once,
- **read, add, edit, and delete comments** on a card.

<Callout type="info">
  An Agent is held to the same rules as a person editing the card. It can only
  assign someone who can work in this Workspace: a member of the Organization,
  or an Agent available here. Labels are stricter than they look. A new card
  with a label the board doesn't have is refused, but an edit or bulk edit drops
  the unknown label and reports success, so the Agent believes it applied it.
  Tell it to read the board's labels first and use their ids, rather than
  inventing one.
</Callout>

This is what makes boards a two-way surface: you arrange the work, and an Agent
— in a Chat or on a schedule via a [Trigger](/building-with-platypus/triggers) —
can keep it moving. Board activity (cards created, moved, updated, or deleted)
can in turn fire **Event** Triggers, so a change on a board can kick off another
Agent run. Deleting a whole column or board is not card activity: the cards it
removes fire nothing.

### When two people move the same card

An Agent run can take minutes, and a board can be open in someone's browser the
whole time — so a card can move while another move is already on its way.
Platypus refuses the later move rather than letting it silently overwrite the
first. The same holds between two Agents working one board at once, when the
moving Agent names the column it last saw the card in: a move made from a stale
reading is refused, and the Agent is told to look again.

<Callout type="warning">
  If you move a card that someone else has moved since your Board last
  refreshed, your move does not apply and the Board reloads to show where the
  card actually is. A dragged card snaps back. If you changed the card's
  **Column** in the card dialog, your other edits to the card are still saved
  and the dialog closes. Nothing else is lost, and you can repeat the move
  against the Board as it now stands.
</Callout>

## Card history

Every card keeps a short record of how it reached its current state: who moved
it, who changed its labels, priority, due date, title or assignee, and when.
Open a card and click **History** at the bottom of the dialog.

One write is one entry, however many fields it touched — an Agent that retitles
a card and bumps its priority in a single edit leaves one line, not two.
Reordering a card inside its column changes nothing the history tracks and
leaves no entry at all.

Two things it deliberately does not record:

- **The description's text.** An entry says the description was edited and who
  edited it, never what it said before. Use a Comment if the previous wording
  matters.
- **Anything that happened to the board rather than the card.** Delete a Label
  at board level and it disappears from every card carrying it, and no card's
  history mentions it. Entries written earlier still show that Label by the
  name it had at the time.

<Callout type="warning">
  A card keeps its **50 most recent** entries. Past that, the oldest are dropped
  — permanently, and with no copy kept anywhere. Treating History as an audit
  trail is the trap: it is there to tell whoever picks the card up next how it
  got here, and a busy card outruns it. If you need a durable record of board
  activity, send `card.*` events to a
  [Webhook](/building-with-platypus/webhooks) and keep them your side. Deleting
  a card deletes its history with it.
</Callout>

An Agent with the **Kanban** tool set can read the same history when it fetches
a card, but only when asked to, so tell it to check how a card has moved before
acting on it.
